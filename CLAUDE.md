# Valor Christian College website (vcc-3)

Static site, no build step. `index.html` at the repo root is the current Home page;
`assets/` holds locally-hosted images, fonts, video, and the page runtime JS.

## Design/content source of truth

`docs/BUILD_BRIEF.md` is the client's 2026 Developer & Designer Build Brief. It defines:

- The final site navigation/sitemap (top-level sections + submenu items)
- Per-page goals, required sections, on-page copy, and CTA language
- Global design direction (concise copy, card layouts, story-driven pages)
- Items still marked "confirm/TBD" (tuition figures, staff roster, CHN URL,
  Populi portal link, Microsoft Bookings link, exact history timeline dates,
  final degree/program inventory) — do not invent values for these; ask or
  use a clearly-marked placeholder.

Read `docs/BUILD_BRIEF.md` before building or editing any page on this site —
it applies to Home and every other page in the sitemap, not just the page
being worked on at any given time.
